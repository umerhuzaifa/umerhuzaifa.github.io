analytical expressions

mean — first moment

E[X] = μ
= ∫ x · p(x|μ,σ²) dx

closed form for Gaussian: = μ

variance — second central moment

Var[X] = σ²
= E[(X − μ)²]

= ∫ (x−μ)² · p(x|μ,σ²) dx

empirical (sample) estimators

sample mean

x̄ = (1/N) Σᵢ xᵢ

E[x̄] = μ → unbiased ✓

sample variance (Bessel corrected)

s² = 1/(N−1) Σᵢ (xᵢ−x̄)²

E[s²] = σ² → unbiased ✓

maximum likelihood estimation

log-likelihood for Gaussian

ℓ(μ,σ²) = −(N/2) ln(2πσ²) − (1/2σ²) Σᵢ (xᵢ − μ)²

MLE mean ∂ℓ/∂μ = 0

μ̂_MLE = (1/N) Σᵢ xᵢ

identical to sample mean x̄

MLE variance ∂ℓ/∂σ² = 0

σ̂²_MLE = (1/N) Σᵢ (xᵢ − μ̂)²

biased: E[σ̂²_MLE] = σ²(N−1)/N
