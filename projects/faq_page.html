Registration

What class do I take next for my major?

Check your program requirements in your Degree Progress Report. In Campus Connect select Academic Progress > Degree Progress > Run Report.

Full-time status is 12 credit hours per term for undergraduates and 8 for graduate students. Undergraduates often take 16 or 18 credit hours to stay on track for graduation (special circumstances may call for a reduced load).

Courses & D2L

Why aren't my classes on D2L yet?

Professors have until the week of classes to open their courses in D2L. Keep checking back, and feel free to reach out to the professor for clarification. Email addresses are in the directory.

Courses & D2L

Why does my class time show "to be announced"?

Your class may be online and asynchronous, which never has a scheduled time. Refer to the syllabus on D2L for more info.
