Research Areas

Machine Learning

Our machine learning research focuses on developing novel algorithms and frameworks for deep learning, with applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. We explore efficient training methods, model interpretability, and transfer learning techniques.

Related Publications:

  • Deep Neural Networks for Image Classification (2024)
  • Efficient Transfer Learning Methods (2023)
  • Interpretable AI: A Survey (2023)

Quantum Computing

We investigate quantum algorithms and their applications to solve complex computational problems. Our work includes quantum error correction, quantum machine learning, and the development of near-term quantum algorithms for practical applications in optimization and simulation.

Related Publications:

  • Quantum Error Correction Codes (2024)
  • NISQ Algorithms for Optimization (2023)
  • Quantum Machine Learning Framework (2022)

Network Science

Our network science research examines complex systems through the lens of graph theory and network analysis. We study social networks, biological networks, and infrastructure systems to understand their structure, dynamics, and resilience properties.

Related Publications:

  • Community Detection in Large-Scale Networks (2024)
  • Network Resilience and Cascading Failures (2023)
  • Dynamics of Information Spread (2023)

Robotics & Automation

We develop intelligent robotic systems with capabilities in perception, planning, and control. Our research spans autonomous navigation, human-robot interaction, and collaborative robotics, with applications in manufacturing, healthcare, and service domains.

Related Publications:

  • Vision-Based Navigation for Autonomous Robots (2024)
  • Human-Robot Collaboration Framework (2023)
  • Learning from Demonstration (2022)
